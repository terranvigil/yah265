# Watch the encodes

Scores only go so far. These players show a yah265 encode and an x265 encode of the same clip, made at the same file size, in motion. Switch between them, drag the split, and jump to the moments where they differ most.

| clip | what to look at |
|---|---|
| [Nocturne, 4K at 60 fps](https://terranvigil.github.io/yah265-compare/nocturne-4k/) | 30 seconds of a grain-heavy dance film at about 64 Mbit/s. Watch the grain and the dancer's skin. |
| [Sparks, 1080p at 60 fps](https://terranvigil.github.io/yah265-compare/sparks-1080p/) | 30 seconds of a construction crew at a low streaming rate. Watch the faces and the hands in the close-ups. |
| [A Wikipedia page, scrolling](https://terranvigil.github.io/yah265-compare/screen-wikipedia/) | Four seconds of screen recording at 1.2 Mbit/s. Watch the text. |

Zoom in to see the difference. At its fit size the player shrinks the picture, which hides detail. Scroll, double-click or press + to zoom, and drag to pan.

All three are on [one page](https://terranvigil.github.io/yah265-compare/). Nocturne needs a fast connection.
