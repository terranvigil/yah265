About the speed

This page runs a WebAssembly build of yah265 on one thread, with none of its SIMD kernels. The native encoder uses NEON, SVE, SSE4.2, AVX2 or AVX-512 and every core you have. On one thread alone, the native build ran 3 to 6 times faster than this page in my tests, and it splits the work over every core. Treat the speed shown here as the slow end for yah265.

The picture on the right is the encoder's own reconstruction, the exact frames a decoder would produce from the stream. So no HEVC playback support is needed to see the result.
