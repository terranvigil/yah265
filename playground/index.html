Download .mp4 Download .hevc

The .mp4 plays in most players and in Safari. The .hevc is the raw stream.

This page runs yah265 on one thread, without its SIMD code. The native encoder ran 3 to 6 times faster on one thread, and it uses all of your cores. So this is the slowest yah265 gets.

The right side shows the exact frames a decoder makes from the stream, so your browser doesn't need HEVC support. VMAF v1 is scored the way the results are, at 1080p display size.

Keys: Space plays or pauses. Left and Right step one frame. Home and End jump to the ends. Esc cancels an encode.
