# yah265 end-credits rows, 2026-11-23

A follow-up to CREDITS-yah265-2026-11-22.md. It adds VMAF v1 to the
credits rows, checks the two titles that read behind on PSNR-Y, and makes
one encoder change. Credits rows are supplementary and don't gate.

Encoder: yah265 build-rel, branch `credits-items` (base 105e07d1), preset
medium. Reference: x265 4.3+1-e9b8812, preset medium, the same binary as
the 11-22 rows. Both at 6 threads, lookahead 20 on both sides. v1 is
vmaf_v1.0.16 3d0h at 10 bits, every frame, CAMBI told the 8-bit encode,
scored at the coded size (every credits cell is 1920 wide).

PSNR-Y (`PL_CRED_V1=0` turns it off). BD is over the five rungs, CRF 20

## Tears of Steel: the speed-cut gate is not built

The proposed fix keeps medium's intra cuts and its skip exit and RDOQ
off on frames that look like these credits. The plan was to build it only
if story cells show the same texture. They don't.

The credits frames have a large black share and very fine texture on the
rest. The lookahead sees that as a high mean intra cost per 8x8 lowres
block (`icb`) over the non-flat share of the picture (flat_pct is the
share of 16x16 blocks under one level of variance). The census below
scanned every frame of every HD-class story cell: tier L at 1080p (all
seven titles, credits excluded), tier M at 1080p, tier C at 1080p, tier
S at 1080p and the eight tier D clips. A frame counts when flat_pct >= 35, icb >=
500, icb over the non-flat share >= 1200 and sc_ratio >= 0.12.

| set | frames scanned | frames that match |
|---|---|---|
| tier L 1080p story, 7 titles | 133,650 | 0 |
| tier M 1080p | 8,687 | 0 |
| tier C 1080p | 15,180 | 0 |
| tier S 1080p | 1,125 | 0 |
| tier D, 8 cells | 3,019 | 0 |
| tears credits, whole span | 1,728 | 485 |
| tears credits, the 11-22 slice | 240 | 185 |
| bbb credits | 3,732 | 1,029 |

Looser bounds start to reach story frames (flat_pct >= 30, icb >= 450,
non-flat >= 900 matches 99 bbb, 103 lash and 20 Sol Levante frames), and
those are a still title card and grain, not this texture. A gate that
fires only on credits would move only a supplementary PSNR-Y row, so it
isn't built.

## stem2: not per-frame overhead

The whole span is 614,716 bytes on ours and 440,462 on x265, 1460 frames
each.

| NAL type | ours count / bytes (each) | x265 count / bytes (each) |
|---|---|---|
| non-reference slices (TRAIL_N, RASL_N) | 729 / 25,194 (35) | 704 / 85,855 (122) |
| reference slices (TRAIL_R, RASL_R) | 725 / 558,488 (770) | 750 / 323,292 (431) |
| CRA and IDR | 6 / 24,933 | 6 / 23,056 |
| VPS, SPS, PPS | 73 | 74 |
| SEI | 172 | 2,330 |

Fixed bytes don't explain it: our non-reference pictures cost a third of
x265's, and our SEI is 2 KB smaller. The weighted-prediction table is a
few bits per P slice. The gap is in the P pictures.

| frame type | ours count / mean bytes | x265 count / mean bytes |
|---|---|---|
| I | 6 / 4,203 | 6 / 4,250 |
| P | 182 / 2,761 | 446 / 620 |
| B | 1,272 / 68 | 1,008 / 137 |

The cards fade in and out of black. We keep the 8-frame B run through
the fades, so each P anchor codes eight frames of change, and P pictures
are 82% of the stream. x265 places a P about every three frames there.
On PSNR-Y that costs us about 20%.

v1 found a second problem. Per frame, at the matched crf:

| frames | ours v1 | x265 v1 |
|---|---|---|
| card frames (934) | 96.94 | 96.35 |
| black frames, mean luma 16.05 or under (526) | 96.54 | 98.97 |

We're ahead on the cards and behind on the black between them. x265
codes that black exactly. We left the last card's ghost on it: on frame
1080, 70 pixels up to 79 levels off, and on others 4-6 levels over 11-22k
pixels, always where the card was. The 64x64 skip gate (item 17) passed
those skips, because its bound is a sum over the node. With the gate off
(`N265_CB64_SKIPGATE=0`) the ghost falls from 1633 to 226 pixels per
black frame, and `--preset slow` (no gate) gives 223.

## The change: an exact skip gate on an all-flat picture

`N265_CB64_FLATPIC` (medium at HD; 0 at UHD and in the other presets):
on a picture whose every 16x16 block is flat, the 64x64 skip gate passes
only a skip with zero residual. A rejected node runs the full search as
it already does. Turning the gate off outright was tried first and
refused: on bbb's cut to black it cost +0.93% BD-v1, because the full
search coded a few black frames at 97 that the gate had coded exactly.

stem2 credits at crf 26.52, one stream each:

| arm | bytes | v1 | v1 on black | ghost px per black frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main | 614,716 | 96.80 | 97.08 | 1633 |
| gate off | 613,768 | 97.37 | 98.53 | 242 |
| exact gate (shipped) | 614,560 | 97.11 | 97.94 | 296 |
| x265 CRF 28 | 440,462 | 97.29 | 98.97 | - |

Where it fires in story cells: all-flat pictures occur in tier L bbb
(52), cosmos (105), sintel (131), stem2 (25, a white frame), meridian
