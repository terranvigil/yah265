# yah265 fades to and from black, 2026-11-24

A follow-up to CREDITS-yah265-2026-11-23.md. That report put most of
stem2's credits gap on P-anchor placement through fades. This one
measures fades first, finds a different cause, and ships one change.

Encoder: yah265 build-rel, preset medium. Reference: x265
4.3+1-e9b8812, preset medium. Both at 6 threads, lookahead 20 on both
sides. BD is VMAF v1 (vmaf_v1.0.16 3d0h, every frame, by
`scripts/v1score.py`'s rules) over CRF 20, 23, 26, 29 and 32 on both
sides.

## What the fades look like

### stem2 credits

The 1460 frames are white cards on black. Each card fades in from black
and out to it. At its matched crf (26.52) against x265 CRF 28:

| frame type | ours count / mean bytes | x265 count / mean bytes |
|---|---|---|
| I | 6 / 4,203 | 6 / 3,843 |
| P | 182 / 2,760 | 446 / 616 |
| B | 1,272 / 68 | 1,008 / 133 |

Over one card (excerpt frames 300-420), ours against x265:

| span | ours P frames, bytes | x265 P frames, bytes |
|---|---|---|
| fade in, 308-330 | 314: 6,542, 322: 10,079, 330: 13,308 | 7 Ps, 1,089-4,946 each |
| card held, 331-382 | 36-70 each | 132-398 each |
| fade out, 383-405 | 386: 7,268, 394: 8,048, 402: 4,022 | 386-402: 134-236 each |

x265's fade-out P frames cost about 150 bytes. Ours cost 4-8 KB. That's a
weighted-prediction gap, not a B-run gap:

- With `--no-weightp` our window codes 59,576 bytes against 59,594 with
  it. Weighted prediction never fired on the fade.
- The estimator found the right map every time: scale 0.67, 0.51, 0.19
  and 0 on the four fade-out anchors, offset 16 x (1 - scale), and the
  lowres SAD down 97-100%.
- The block test refused it. It signals a map only when the map improves
  at least 40% of the 8x8 lowres blocks. A fade to black leaves black
  black, so the 93% of the picture that is background tied, and the map
  "won" 7% of blocks.
- A least-squares fit at full resolution agrees the fade is linear: on
  the 80-to-88 anchor pair, 4,707 of 49,610 card pixels miss the
  weighted prediction by more than 3 levels. At distance 4 (80 to 84)
  it's 4,868, so a shorter run wouldn't shrink the residual either.

### The corpus and story fades

| cell | what fades | main BD-v1 vs x265 |
|---|---|---|
| fade_cif | the whole picture, with motion | -14.09% |
| xfade_720p | a dissolve | -8.92% |
| sintel_720p | trailer dissolves and fades | -8.52% |

On fade_cif the picture moves, so every block changes, the map passes
the block test (74-100%), and the b-adapt motion rule already ends the
run every second frame. The full-picture fades were never the problem.

## The change

`N265_WP_TIES` (medium, every class): a luma map whose fixed point is at
or under level 24 leaves out of the block test the blocks it doesn't
change. The fixed point of w x + o is o / (1 - w). A fade to or from
black holds black, so this is the fade-to-black case and nothing else.
Chroma maps and maps anchored anywhere else are judged as before.

On stem2's credits window the fade-out maps now read 97-100% of the
blocks they change. 0 turns it off.

### What was tried first

- Leaving ties out for every map, chroma included. It moved 5-43 maps
  per tier L title, many of them chroma maps, plus fades toward white.
  Narrowed to black-anchored luma maps, a tier L title moves 0-16.
- A shorter B run. With the weight fixed, `--bframes 3` over stem2's
  credits reads -33.95% BD-v1 against x265 where 7 reads -37.54%. At a
  fixed crf on the card window, 3 and 1 code within 4% of 7's bytes at
  a lower PSNR-Y. No fade-aware B-run rule is built, because nothing was
  left for it to win.
- An 80% bar on the tie-free share, to keep weak maps out. It would turn
  off all six of meridian 28121's maps (-2.23%) and keep meridian 29691's
  one (+0.61%), so the share doesn't sort good maps from bad ones.
